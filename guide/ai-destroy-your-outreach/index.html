AI Is Going To Destroy Your Outreach

Unless you make these changes.

Who this is for

Anyone booking calls through outbound who has noticed cold email working worse than it used to.

Who am I? I'm Dan James. I scaled a previous content agency from zero to 30K a month in 60 days by booking whale meetings, and my offer now is guaranteeing whale clients for other businesses.

What is happening

  • AI has made outbound easy for everyone. Anyone can spin up a cold email campaign or automation sequence in Claude Code today. Whole companies (like ColdIQ) now live inside it.
  • Social platforms are fighting back with AI slop filters. Email and DMs have no police, so nobody will stop the flood.
  • Every channel gets attacked: email, video, cold calling. More senders means lower conversion rates.
  • On sales calls I hear it every week: "one of the reasons we wanted to speak to you is our cold email isn't working as well."

The one thing AI can't attack: human connection

Until robots are indistinguishable from people, real human connection can't be copied by someone in their mum's basement. So put your eggs there. Here are three ways to do it.

1. The strategy document

A 30 to 50+ page document where you research the prospect in granular detail and explain exactly how you'll help.

  1. Find the problem. Go through their whole funnel. Links broken? No emails arriving?
  2. Tie it to money. "Your automations aren't running, so clients come to calls colder, so your close rate drops, so you lose money."
  3. Make a no-brainer offer. "I'll build your first automation for $0, then we can talk about working together."
  4. Add social proof. The problem, what we did, what happened, the result (for example: email consumption from 0% to 75%, plus 150K in extra cash collected).
  5. Give real deliverables. The test: if they paid you $5,000 to $10,000, what would you give them? Give that for free.

Build it as a webpage or a Gamma doc, not a Google Doc, so you know when they open it. I've booked hundreds of calls with this.

2. A personal gift in the post

You need their work address. Then address it to the key decision maker.

Example: your prospect is a huge Rafael Nadal fan. Find a signed Nadal racket on eBay or Etsy (around $150). Add a handwritten note: "We hope you love this racket as much as we'd love to work with you. Call this number, scan this QR code or message me on WhatsApp when you receive this." Add a photo of you smiling (with your family if you have one). A face goes a long way.

They'll show their partner, their family and their friends, and they'll call you. The gift grabs attention first. You sell second. Selin Kocalar did this with rugs sent to startups like Cluely and it went viral.

3. Events

Put on events or go to them. Yes, there's travel and cost, but being in person speeds the sales process up like crazy. Jordan Platten's biggest regret about events was not starting sooner, because he always left with clients.

  • Go where the person you want to work with will be.
  • Bring a printed 50-page proposal in a binder: "We came because we thought you might be here."
  • Even if you meet someone lower down, they become your champion and take it to their boss. Mid-market and enterprise sales is all about creating champions.

The takeaway

While everyone else sends Claude Code emails, turn up in person or send something bespoke that makes them think "this is for us specifically." Whales need that before they'll even have a conversation.
