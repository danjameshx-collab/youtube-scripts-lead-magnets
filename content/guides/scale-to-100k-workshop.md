# Start with better business thinking

If your profit margins are bad, outreach isn't worth the effort and the rejection. Make the juice worth the squeeze first.

**Build a social proof deck.** Every time anyone says anything good about you, put it in a deck. When someone on a sales call says "I don't really trust you," just scroll through it. There are four types of proof: case study interviews, video testimonials, screenshots and written testimonials.

---

# The 3 mistakes that stop you scaling

1. **Using AI for no reason.** Playing with Claude Code feels like progress, but nine times out of ten it has nothing to do with your constraint. Your job is to get customers. Use AI to help you make content, run ads, onboard clients and do outreach, not for its own sake. AI can even hurt you: my handwritten LinkedIn posts get 10,000 impressions while a friend's AI posts get no comments.
2. **Not working on your number one constraint.** Every business has one thing holding it back: lead generation, sales or fulfilment. **If you don't know what yours is, it's demand: get more leads.** Never stop a profitable action. A waiting list is better than turning customers away to build a system.
3. **Not putting in the work.** Going from 10K to 20K a month is a 100% increase, and it's brutal. Someone in your niche is working like crazy to beat you. If you want a lifestyle business that's fine, just know the game you're playing.

**Quick check:** have you posted 10+ long-form YouTube videos, 50+ short-form videos and 400+ pieces of outreach this year? If not, that's the gap.

# There are only 3 ways to get customers

| Way | What it is | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid ads** | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google | D2C and e-com. B2B only once you're at around 40K a month |
| **Outbound** | Cold email, cold DMs, in person | The bigger your ticket size, the more it matters (roughly 1.5K+) |
| **Inbound** | Content, events, masterminds, networking | Everyone, always |

**Why B2B under 40K a month shouldn't run ads:** in B2B it can cost $800 to $8,000 to win one customer from ads. Below 40K a month you don't have the cash to keep that going. **Under 40K a month, B2B = content and outreach. D2C and e-com = content and ads.**

# You must make content

- When you do outreach, people search you straight away. Content builds trust and lowers what it costs to win a customer. **You are an internet scammer until people see your face.**
- Hormozi could walk into a room and charge 5K for a workshop because of how much trust his content built.
- Don't know what to make? Find someone in your niche whose content works and copy the topics. Start with 10 kilos, not 150.
- **YouTube is the most powerful platform** because of watch time. Google's 7-11-4 idea: 7 hours of content, 11 touch points, 4 platforms.
- For agencies, make your content about the money your client makes, not views or brand visibility.

---

# Get maximum social proof

Given two identical products, everyone buys the one with 1,900 reviews over the one with 3. People hesitate on sales calls because they want to lower their risk. More social proof, more content and more authority = more certainty.

- **Video client interviews are the best kind.** My LinkedIn profile is client interview after client interview, plus a deck of around 700 screenshots.
- The more interviews, the bigger the business: I have 7 at 40K a month, Evan has 9 at 200K a month, my mentor Daniel Fazio has 52 interviews and 519 screenshots at $1.4 million a month.
- Clients won't give you one? Bribe them: 10% off, a free consulting session.
- All the best ads just use social proof.

---

# You must have a good offer

An offer is a clear benefit someone gets from your service. **Make your client the hero** (Luke Skywalker, not you). Nobody cares that you "do webinars" or "do automations." They care about the dream outcome.

- **Dream state vs nightmare state:** test both, and on sales calls move between what happens if they do this and what happens if they don't.
- **Create the gap** (Cole Gordon): why everything they've tried hasn't worked, and why yours will.
- **Hormozi's value equation:** big dream outcome, high belief it will work, fast results, little effort or sacrifice. "Lose 30 pounds without running on the treadmill."
- **Guarantees win:** Fazio has never seen a B2B offer over 100K a month without one. Mine: "We guarantee a meeting with a whale client of your choice by sending the most incredible creative outbound on earth, done for you, refund if we miss." I've refunded zero people.
- Scared of refunds? Add stipulations (terms the client must meet, like attending the calls). 50% more sales with 3 to 8% refunds still makes you far more money.
- Your market isn't "sophisticated" enough for a strong offer? Make it sophisticated yourself and take everyone.
- See an offer working in your market? Take it. Mine was copied from someone who was killing it.

# Set targets and stick to them like your life depends on it

Business is a list of inputs. I hate the gym but I've gone five times a week for 14 years. Set your content and outreach targets (I batch YouTube and LinkedIn in one day a month) and never miss them. Start small and add weight over time.

# Know your numbers

These numbers exist whether you track them or not.

| Metric | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **LTV** (lifetime value) | Total a client pays you over the whole time they stay | 1K a month for 10 months = 10K |
| **COGS** (cost of goods sold) | What it costs to deliver for them | Staff, time, onboarding: 4K |
| **LTGP** (lifetime gross profit) | LTV minus COGS. Better than LTV | 10K minus 4K = 6K |
| **CAC** (cost to acquire a customer) | What it costs to win one client | Thousands in B2B, $5 to $25 in D2C |
| **LTGP to CAC ratio** | Profit per client vs cost to win them. Bigger = you can scale | 2 to 1 is awful, 8 to 1 is a business |
| **Gross margin** | Profit after delivery costs, as a % | Aim for 80% |
| **Net profit** | What lands in your bank after every cost | |
| **Churn** | % of clients who leave each month | Make people stay longer |

**Why 80% gross margins:** on 100K revenue, 66% margins and 54K of running costs leaves you 12K. At 80% margins and 26K of costs you keep far more. Better offers, brand and authority raise your margins.

**Lower your CAC:** better offers, brand, higher authority, affiliate programmes, better targeting.

**Raise your LTGP:** increase your price (the strongest lever), lower delivery costs, get people buying more often, cross-sells, upsells and down-sells.
