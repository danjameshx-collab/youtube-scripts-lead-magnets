# Outreach That's Impossible To Ignore

The step-by-step process for outreach that whale clients can't ignore.

---

# Who this is for

Anyone who wants a higher response rate. A higher response rate means more calls, and more calls means more money.

**Who am I?** I'm Dan James. I scaled a former agency from zero to 40K a month in about 30 days by signing massive whale clients. Now I book meetings with very large companies like ListKit.

**The goal:** reactions where prospects show their partner and post about you publicly, so your outreach builds your brand.

---

# Step 1: Pick the right names

- Make a shortlist of **25** people or companies you want to work with.
- Cut it to your **top 5**.
- Pick the one at the top: the one you feel you can close best.

---

# Step 2: Check the money

Because you'll spend more time and money per client, it costs you more to win each one. So the deal has to be worth it. Estimate it: "this is worth 100K, maybe 150K." **If you can't estimate what a client is worth, you haven't done enough research.**

---

# Step 3: Research in three ways

1. **Personal.** Who are the key decision makers as people? Check LinkedIn, interviews and their website. You can even message their employees: "We're putting together a proposal for X. Could you tell me a bit about your boss?" You'll be surprised who replies.
2. **Business.** The company (or the branch you're targeting). What have they done, what mistakes have they made, where are the opportunities? Do a SWOT analysis (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats). Look hardest at what's going wrong. If you don't know how you'll help by now, you're stuck.
3. **Strategic.** Their short-term goals today and their long-term goals. Most whale outreach fails on timing. **Line up what they're doing today with where they want to be in five years, and put yourself in the bridge.**

---

# Step 4: Find the signal

The signal is a small detail nobody else found because they didn't put the work in: a CEO interview, a LinkedIn post or comment.

**Example:** a decision maker posts that they want to sign bigger companies in Birmingham. Your outreach: "From my research, I know you want to sign larger companies in Birmingham. Here's exactly how we'll help you do that."

As Jill Konrath says in *Selling to Big Companies*: the better you understand the organisation, the easier selling becomes.

---

# Step 5: Pick your medium

The strongest options: an in-person stunt or meeting at an event, a physical good, an outreach movie. A strategy document is the weakest of the four.

**Example:** Jane Doe at Amazon London loves Rafael Nadal. Send a signed racket: "Jane, we hope you love this racket as much as we'd love to work with you. Give this number a call when you receive it. We'd love to chat about how we can help with your Q2 supply chain initiatives." Jane shows her partner and her kids, and posts a photo on LinkedIn.

**The test:** if you could send the same thing to anyone else on earth, even someone else in the same company, you've failed. Use their full name, things only about them, even photos.

---

# Step 6: Plan and produce

What are we sending, why, how do we buy it, how do we get their address? This is mostly logistics, and the more research you did, the easier it is. AI can help with the research.

---

# Step 7: Make sure they see it

As Kobe Bryant said, the job's not finished until they actually see it.

- **Post it publicly** if they're big on LinkedIn, YouTube, X or Instagram. Tag them and their team. People will message them about it.
- **Keep following up** by email, call or a strategy doc: "I made something incredible for you. I know I'm pestering you, but please don't make me fly to your headquarters, because I will."

---

# Step 8: Milk it for social proof

Post about the outreach you made. Other business owners will message you: "What you did for them is incredible, I'd love to chat." Getting inbound leads from outbound is unheard of, but it works.
